How the Policy Engine Works

  • RBAC: Checks whether your assigned role has the required permission.
  • Time-based Access: Restricts policy-protected actions to 9 AM–5 PM.
  • ABAC: Applies department-based access rules where configured.
  • Audit Logging: Records access decisions for review.
